Expansion guideline

Approve a regional expansion only after all checks are satisfied for a sustained planning period.

1. At least 35% projected margin

Revenue after payment fees must cover server and reserved egress costs with at least a 35% margin.

2. Three months of regional coverage

Reserve must cover three months of the estimated regional operating cost before a new node is committed.

3. Human approval and capacity review

Validate customer demand, redundancy, monitoring, data rules, and hardware limits. Never migrate a customer automatically based on this card alone.
