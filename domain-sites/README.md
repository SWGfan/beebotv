# Beebo domain sites

These two self-contained landing pages are ready to deploy as the document root for their matching domains:

- `beeborelay.com/index.html`
- `beebovpn.com/index.html`
- `beebospace.com/index.html`

All Beebo products are under construction. The pages do not offer checkout, downloads, community registration, or availability promises for services that have not launched. Before publishing, map each domain to its own web root and add TLS certificates for both its root and `www` hostnames.

The footer links connect the products without describing them as separate legal companies. They should remain under the same privacy policy, terms, and support contact unless separate legal entities are actually formed.
